RGBControl controls lighting on compatible Gigabyte motherboard USB RGB controllers.
It can set a fixed color, show several colors at once, and play moving themes.

Hardware access
  * Reads the USB lighting controller's identity and color calibration.
  * Sends lighting commands to that controller and its connected RGB headers.
  * Can turn those connected lights on or off. It does not control fan or pump speed.

Data and network access
  * Saves your theme settings and the latest service status in ProgramData\RGBControl.
  * Does not connect to the internet, collect telemetry, or require an account.

Installation choice
  * Background service: starts with Windows, plays themes after you close the app,
    and handles sleep, hibernation, and shutdown lighting events. The service runs
    as Windows LocalSystem so it can access the controller before sign-in.
  * App only: themes play while the RGB Control window is open. No background
    service is installed or started.

The app is installed in Program Files\RGBControl. A Start menu shortcut is always
created. You can choose a desktop shortcut on the next page. Windows Settings
will list RGBControl so you can uninstall it later.
